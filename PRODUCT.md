## Users

- **Primary — Prospective & Staying Guests (`Primary Guest` and accompanying `Guests`)**: International and domestic travelers across six supported locales (`en`, `es`, `fr`, `it`, `de`, `ja`)—including families, couples, and remote workers/digital nomads—evaluating, booking, or arriving at Property `1707` or Property `1606` in Edificio Salguero Sunset (Playa Salguero, Santa Marta, Colombia).
  - **Pre-booking job**: Evaluate beachfront accommodations, compare real-time seasonal availability and itemized pricing in Settlement Currency (`COP`) with optional reference currency views, submit pre-booking `Inquiries`, or complete a zero-commission `Direct Reservation` via Mercado Pago (`Card`, `PSE`, or `Cash Voucher`).
  - **Pre-arrival & check-in job**: Whether arriving via a `Direct Reservation` (`ovf_*`), an `External Reservation` (`res-abnb-*` from Airbnb), or a `Manual Reservation` (`res-man-*`), complete the mandatory statutory `Guest Registry` (identities, ages, `Personal Data Authorization`, `Minor Guardian Attestation` when applicable, and vehicle license plates) so front-desk clearance is issued and the `Guest Guide` unlocks temporal `Access Credentials` (`0XXXXXX#` smart-lock PIN, Wi-Fi, and `Assigned Parking Space`).
- **Secondary — Admin Users (`admin` / `superadmin` / operators)**: Property owners and operators using the subdomain-isolated `Admin Console` (`admin.oceanviewflats.com`) to manage reservations, `Seasonal Rate Tiers`, `Maintenance Blocks`, iCalendar `Channel Sync`, `Condominium Clearance Sync` with Huésped Manager, `Operational Expenses`, `Financial Reports`, and quarterly FONTUR `Parafiscal Settlements`. Design work in this repository focuses primarily on the public Guest Portal (`oceanviewflats.com`) unless an `Admin Console` surface is explicitly targeted.

## Product Purpose

OceanViewFlats (`oceanviewflats.com`) is the direct booking platform and self-service guest fulfillment system for two owner-operated beachfront properties in Santa Marta, Colombia: **Property 1707** (`RNT #268781`, `Assigned Parking Space` Bay 95) and **Property 1606** (`RNT #270413`, `Assigned Parking Space` Bay 87).

It exists to let guests book directly without third-party OTA commissions while automating the end-to-end statutory and building compliance required before physical entry at Edificio Salguero Sunset. Success means:
1. Prospective visitors clearly understand each Property's distinct offering, verify real-time availability and authoritative `COP` quotes, and complete `Direct Reservations` with confidence.
2. Confirmed guests from any `Booking Source` complete the mandatory `Guest Registry` smoothly on mobile before arrival, triggering automated `Condominium Clearance Sync` and unlocking their `Guest Guide` and `Access Credentials` without manual host intervention.
3. Personal data and access credentials remain strictly protected and automatically redacted once a stay becomes a `Concluded Reservation`.

## Positioning

Unlike generic OTA listings or brochure rental sites that punt check-in logistics to WhatsApp threads, OceanViewFlats unifies commission-free `Direct Reservations` in `COP` with a compliance-gated fulfillment pipeline purpose-built for Colombian tourism law and Edificio Salguero Sunset's front-desk procedures:
- **Authoritative COP Quote Engine**: Evaluates night-by-night `Seasonal Rate Tiers`, enforces minimum stay rules, and itemizes per-property `Cleaning Fees` separately from the on-site `Building Registration Fee` (`20,000 COP` per person payable at reception).
- **Hold-Window Checkout Protection**: Coordinates concurrency-safe `Pending Reservations` with a 30-minute `Standard Hold Window` for interactive digital checkouts (`Card`, `PSE`) and a 72-hour `Voucher Hold Window` for offline `Cash Vouchers` (e.g., Efecty), backed by `Resurrection Defense`.
- **Registry-Gated Access Fulfillment**: Hardware-agnostic temporal `Access Credentials` (`0XXXXXX#` door PIN, Wi-Fi credentials, and deeded parking bay instructions) remain cryptographically and server-side locked until the `Guest Registry` is submitted and cleared, then automatically suppress and redact after `23:59:59 COT` on the `Check-out Date` (`Concluded Reservation`).

## Operating Context

- **Physical Environment**: Edificio Salguero Sunset (`Calle 26 # 2-80, Playa Salguero, Santa Marta, Magdalena, Colombia`). Guests often interact with the `Guest Registry` and `Guest Guide` while in transit (airports, taxis, or the building lobby) over variable mobile connections.
- **Condominium Reception Workflow**: Front-desk staff verify guest authorization via the `Condominium Administration Portal` (Huésped Manager) using the `Condominium Clearance Number` (`consecutivo`) generated by OceanViewFlats' `Condominium Clearance Sync`, and collect the statutory `20,000 COP` per-person `Building Registration Fee` upon arrival.
- **Regulatory Environment**:
  - Registered Colombian tourism operator (`Data Controller`: OceanViewFlats — Manuel Enrique Herrera, `RNT #268781` and `RNT #270413`).
  - Colombian Statutory Law 1581 of 2012 & Decree 1377 of 2013 (`Data Processing Policy`, prior and express `Personal Data Authorization`, XSalsa20-Poly1305 encrypted guest payloads at rest, `PII Redaction` 90 days post-checkout or upon ARCO suppression).
  - Colombian Law 679 of 2001 (ESCNNA hospitality prevention requiring explicit `Minor Guardian Attestation` whenever any registered `Guest` is under 18 or holds a `Tarjeta de Identidad` or `Registro Civil`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Zero-Hydration Multilingual SSG Architecture**:
  - Public Portal pages (`home`, `Oceanview1707`, `Oceanview1606`, `contact`, `registry`, `guide`, `privacy`, `booking-success`, `booking-pending`, `booking-failure`, `404`) are pre-rendered at build time via React 19 SSG (`render.tsx`) into static HTML across all 6 locales (`en`, `es`, `fr`, `it`, `de`, `ja`) in `dist/`.
  - Client-side interactivity (`public/js/`) uses lightweight Vanilla JS without React client hydration, reading localized strings exclusively via `window.t(key, params, fallback)` and runtime configuration (`lang`, `assetPrefix`, `apiBase`) via `window.getPageConfig()` from `#ovf-page-config`.
  - Relative `assetPrefix` (`./` or `../`) must propagate to every local link and asset reference.
- **On-Demand Third-Party SDKs**: External payment scripts (`sdk.mercadopago.com/js/v2`) are never loaded in global HTML shells; they load asynchronously on active checkout intent.
- **Theme & Configuration Centralization**: React components in `src/components/` and `src/pages/` remain style-agnostic; visual tokens, layout classes, and endpoint/asset constants live in `src/constants/theme.ts` and `src/constants/config.ts`.
- **Canonical Domain Language**: Copy, identifiers, and documentation must honor the ubiquitous language defined in `CONTEXT.md` (e.g., `Property` as the canonical domain entity, `Check-in Date`, `Check-out Date`, `Nightly Rate`, `Quote`, `Cleaning Fee`, `Building Registration Fee`, `Settlement Currency`, `Guest Registry`, `Guest Guide`, `Access Credential`, `Primary Guest`, `Maximum Occupancy` of 6 Guests unless an `Occupancy Override` is active).

## Product Principles

1. **Radical Transparency Before Commitment**: Every fee (`Nightly Rate`, `Cleaning Fee`, and the on-site `20,000 COP` per-person `Building Registration Fee`), hold window, and statutory registration requirement is disclosed clearly before a guest pays or arrives.
2. **Compliance as Frictionless Hospitality**: Mandatory Colombian legal steps (`Guest Registry`, `Personal Data Authorization`, `Minor Guardian Attestation`, and `Condominium Clearance Sync`) should feel like a reassuring, well-guided concierge check-in rather than bureaucratic red tape.
3. **Zero-Hydration Speed on Travel Connections**: Guest-facing surfaces must load fast and work reliably on roaming mobile connections in transit—shipping static HTML, minimal Vanilla JS, and on-demand payment SDKs only when needed.
4. **Six-Language Parity Without Drift**: Every guest-facing flow, validation message, and legal notice works with equal clarity across English, Spanish, French, Italian, German, and Japanese from a single build-time translation contract.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.1 AA+ Compliance**: Maintain high text and control contrast, semantic landmarks, keyboard operability, explicit form labels, and screen-reader-friendly status announcements across checkout, `Guest Registry`, and `Guest Guide` flows.
- **Older-Traveler & Multi-Generational Readability**: Use generous tap targets, clear typographic hierarchy, unambiguous date/currency formatting (`COP`), and straightforward step-by-step form progression suitable for older travelers and family group leaders.
- **Low-Bandwidth & Mobile Roaming Resilience**: Optimize for travelers on throttled cellular data or lobby Wi-Fi—keep payloads lightweight (`webp` imagery, zero React runtime bundle, deferred third-party SDKs) and preserve form state gracefully during intermittent network drops.
- **Multilingual Expansion Tolerance**: Layouts, buttons, badges, and tables must accommodate text expansion in German, French, Spanish, and Italian as well as CJK line-breaking in Japanese without clipping or horizontal overflow.
